Scenario-based consulting

Certification: Scenario-Based Consulting

This section presents real-world scenarios you would encounter as a LiveKit consultant. Each question requires you to synthesize knowledge across multiple domains — architecture, agents, telephony, frontends, and operations — to recommend a solution and defend your reasoning.

Consulting mindset

These questions simulate client conversations. The best answer is not just technically correct — it considers the client's context, budget, timeline, and team capabilities. A perfect architecture that the client cannot operate is worse than a simpler one they can maintain.
